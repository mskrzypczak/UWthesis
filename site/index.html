UWthesis

A LaTeX class for doctoral dissertations at the University of Warsaw.

Purpose

A doctoral dissertation at the University of Warsaw begins with a number of formal pages: the title page, the statements of the supervisors and of the author, the keywords, and the abstracts in Polish and in English. The class uw-thesis typesets all of them from a few commands in the preamble, and otherwise behaves like the standard class report.

The layout is aligned with the doctoral dissertation template of the Faculty of Mathematics, Informatics, and Mechanics. Formal requirements differ between the units of the University and change over time, so please check the result against the rules that apply to you.

Possibilities

  • The whole front matter with one command. \makefrontmatter prints the title page, the statements of the supervisors and of the author, the keywords, both abstracts and the table of contents.
  • English or Polish. With the option english the title page is in English and the remaining formal pages are bilingual, Polish and English; with polish everything is in Polish.
  • Several supervisors. Every \addSupervisor and \addCosupervisor adds a person to the title page, together with a separate statement with a place for the date and the signature.
  • Your unit and discipline. The faculty or doctoral school, the field, the discipline and the place are set with \faculty, \fieldEN / \fieldPL, \disciplineEN / \disciplinePL and \place.
  • Standard LaTeX underneath. A4 paper, 12pt Times-like font, 3 cm margins, one-and-a-half line spacing and a two-sided layout; your own packages, theorem environments and BibTeX work as in report. The class is compiled with pdfLaTeX.

All options and commands are described in the manual.

Quick start

Keep uw-thesis.cls and the logo files next to the main file of the thesis:

\documentclass[english]{uw-thesis}

\titleEN{Title of the doctoral dissertation}
\titlePL{Polski tytuł rozprawy doktorskiej}
\author{Your Full Name}
\date{June 2026}

\faculty{Doctoral School of Exact and Natural Sciences}
\disciplineEN{Computer and Information Sciences}
\disciplinePL{informatyka}

\addSupervisor{Prof.~Main Supervisor}
\addCosupervisor{Dr~Assistant Supervisor}

\keywords{keyword one; keyword two; keyword three}
\abstractPL{Tutaj wstaw streszczenie po polsku.}
\abstractEN{Put the English abstract here.}

\begin{document}
\makefrontmatter

\chapter{Introduction}
Start the thesis here.
\end{document}
